        reactToTrackSegment \newline OccupancyChange &
        Automatically enforces interlocking rules when track occupancy changes. Reacts to safety-critical transitions. \\\hline

        handleCriticalFailure&
        Initiates system freeze and emits safety violation alerts when a module fails validation or encounters inconsistency. \\\hline

        recordResponseTime &
        Tracks and stores performance timings. Raises performance warnings if safety checks exceed time thresholds. \\\hline

        initialize&
        Prepares internal modules, validates database connectivity, and transitions the interlocking engine to an operational state. \\\hline
